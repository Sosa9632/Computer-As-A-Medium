### Task 1 — Health pack

[`HealthPack.cs`](HealthPack.cs) and [`PlayerHealth.cs`](PlayerHealth.cs) add a green pack that restores up to 10 health and disappears when used.

[`Trap.cs`](Trap.cs) makes a yellow block pop up when the player takes damage from the spikes.

### Task 3 — Mana

[`PlayerAttack.cs`](PlayerAttack.cs) displays mana and spends 10 mana per R press to swing the sword.
